## 2. Immediate Next Step: Build Dashboard 2 (Retention & Turnover Risk)

The user is in Tableau Cloud Web Authoring. Follow these exact steps to complete Dashboard 2:

### Step 2.1: Open Workbook for Editing
1. In Tableau Cloud, open workbook `HR_Analytics_Demo` → click **Edit** (top-right).

### Step 2.2: Build Sheet 4 — "Headcount by Dept"
1. Click the **New Worksheet** icon (`+` sheet icon at the bottom tab bar).
2. From the Data pane:
   - Drag `NAME` (under `DEPARTMENTS`) to the **Rows** shelf.
   - Drag `EMP_ID` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Columns** shelf.
   - Right-click the `EMP_ID` pill on Columns → choose **Measure** → **Count (Distinct)** (`CNTD(EMP_ID)`).
   - Drag `TERM_DATE` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Filters** card.
   - In the filter dialog, select **Special** → pick **Null values only** (this isolates active employees).
3. Double-click the sheet tab at the bottom → rename to `Headcount by Dept`.

### Step 2.3: Build Sheet 5 — "Exits by Dept"
1. Click the **New Worksheet** icon (`+`).
2. From the Data pane:
   - Drag `NAME` (under `DEPARTMENTS`) to the **Rows** shelf.
   - Drag `EMP_ID` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Columns** shelf → set measure to **Count (Distinct)**.
   - Drag `TERM_DATE` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Filters** card.
   - Select **Special** → pick **Non-null values only** (this isolates terminated/resigned employees).
3. Double-click the sheet tab at the bottom → rename to `Exits by Dept`.

### Step 2.4: Build Sheet 6 — "Hires Over Time"
1. Click the **New Worksheet** icon (`+`).
2. From the Data pane:
   - Drag `HIRE_DATE` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Columns** shelf. Click the pill dropdown and choose continuous **Month** (e.g. `May 2024`).
   - Drag `EMP_ID` (under `EMPLOYEES`) to the **Rows** shelf → set measure to **Count (Distinct)**.
3. Double-click the sheet tab at the bottom → rename to `Hires Over Time`.

### Step 2.5: Assemble Dashboard 2 — "Retention & Turnover Risk Dashboard"
1. Click the **New Dashboard** icon (icon with 4 squares at bottom).
2. On the bottom-left pane, check **"Show dashboard title"**. Double-click the title and name it:
   `Retention & Turnover Risk Dashboard`
3. From the Sheets list on the left:
   - Drag `Headcount by Dept` onto the canvas.
   - Drag `Exits by Dept` right next to it (side-by-side).
   - Drag `Hires Over Time` underneath both charts across the full width.
4. Double-click the dashboard tab at the bottom → rename to `Retention & Turnover Risk Dashboard`.

### Step 2.6: Save / Publish
1. Click **File** → **Save** (or blue **Publish** / **Save** button at top).
2. Ensure **"Show sheets as tabs"** is checked.
