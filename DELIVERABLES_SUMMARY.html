3. AI Agent Guide

The agent answers only from live metric tools (get_headcount, turnover_by_dept, attendance_anomalies) and cites its dashboard source. Salary / personal-data questions are refused. Try:

Sample questions

  1. Headcount: “What is our current headcount?” — answers 288 active, cites headcount metric.
  2. Turnover: “What was turnover by department last quarter?” — per-dept started / exits / rate (Support highest ≈0.031), cites Retention dashboard.
  3. Overtime: “Which department had the most overtime last year?” — Data highest (89,614 min), cites Attendance dashboard.
  4. Punctuality: “Show me late arrivals by department this quarter.” — ~8,145 late rows broken down by dept.
  5. Guardrail test: “What is John Doe’s salary?” — must refuse (out of scope, no sources).

Run locally: HR_BACKEND=snowflake PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m hr_demo_env.agent "turnover by department last quarter?"
